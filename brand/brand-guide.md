# EnglishTale Brand Guide

## Brand positioning
EnglishTale teaches practical English through original, entertaining animated stories. The viewer follows recurring characters through everyday situations and learns phrases in context.

## Brand promise
**Learn English Through Stories.**

Each episode should:
1. Tell a complete, enjoyable story.
2. Teach a small set of useful phrases.
3. Model natural pronunciation and conversational rhythm.
4. Give the viewer a chance to listen, repeat, and answer.

## Audience
Primary initial audience: Persian-speaking beginner and elementary English learners (A1–A2). The format should remain globally understandable: English dialogue, clear English captions, concise Persian explanations. Future language editions can be produced as separate localized versions rather than mixing many languages into one video.

## Visual identity
- Primary: deep navy / petrol blue — `#123B52`
- Accent: warm golden yellow — `#F4C542`
- Background: warm cream — `#FFF4DF`
- Secondary: sky blue — `#69B7D2`
- Text: near-black navy — `#172A35`
- Style: original, polished 3D family-friendly animation; expressive faces; soft cinematic light; readable silhouettes.
- Avoid copying a specific studio, film, or copyrighted character style. Use the original style description above.

## Typography
Use a clean, highly legible sans-serif. Suggested options: Nunito, Poppins, or a system sans-serif. Verify font licensing before embedding or commercial use.

## Thumbnail system
- 16:9 landscape.
- One clear emotional moment.
- Main character large and readable on mobile.
- Strong contrast and one short English phrase, ideally 2–5 words.
- Do not clutter with many words, flags, logos, or tiny details.
- Generate the artwork without text, then add correct text manually in an editor.

## Recurring structure
1. Cold open / story problem.
2. Story in natural English.
3. Short Persian explanation of selected phrases.
4. Listen-and-repeat practice.
5. Quick quiz.
6. Friendly invitation to watch the next story.

## Voice identity
- Narrator: warm, clear, friendly American English.
- Leo: young male voice, curious and slightly nervous at first.
- Emma: young female voice, calm, supportive, upbeat.
- Airport agent: polite, professional adult voice.
- Ollie: cheerful guide voice; use sparingly.
- Persian explanations: clear, natural Persian, moderate pace.

## Naming convention
`ET_E001_SC03_LEO_001.wav`
`ET_E001_SC03_BG_001.png`
`ET_E001_SC03_ANIM_001.mp4`

Use episode, scene, character/asset, and take number in filenames.
