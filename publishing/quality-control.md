# Publishing Quality-Control Checklist

## Story and teaching
- [ ] Story has a clear beginning, problem, resolution, and ending.
- [ ] English is natural and suitable for the stated level.
- [ ] Afghan Dari translation preserves meaning and sounds natural.
- [ ] Every taught phrase is useful and correctly explained.
- [ ] Quiz answer is unambiguous.
- [ ] No factual travel/security advice is presented as universal.

## Visuals
- [ ] Leo, Emma, and Ollie match approved reference designs.
- [ ] No accidental logos, trademarks, watermarks, or text artifacts.
- [ ] Hands, faces, props, and backgrounds have been inspected.
- [ ] Captions do not cover important faces.
- [ ] Thumbnail is readable at mobile size.

## Audio
- [ ] Every English line has been checked for pronunciation.
- [ ] No clipped words, awkward pauses, or abrupt edits.
- [ ] English and Afghan Dari levels are balanced.
- [ ] Music is quiet under dialogue.
- [ ] All sound and voice assets have appropriate usage rights.

## Subtitles
- [ ] English captions match the spoken audio.
- [ ] Afghan Dari translation is accurate.
- [ ] Timing has been reviewed manually.
- [ ] Spelling, punctuation, and line breaks are clean.
- [ ] Captions stay within safe margins.

## Rights and monetization readiness
- [ ] Commercial-use terms checked for every AI tool and asset.
- [ ] License evidence or source details saved.
- [ ] Music and sound effects are cleared for YouTube use.
- [ ] No unlicensed character, story, image, or music imitation.
- [ ] Video adds original educational value and is not repetitive template spam.
- [ ] Audience designation and synthetic-content disclosure reviewed according to current YouTube rules.

## Final export
- [ ] 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps.
- [ ] Audio checked on phone speaker and headphones.
- [ ] Full video watched from beginning to end.
- [ ] Title, description, thumbnail, playlist, and end screen checked.
