## Description

Learn practical British English through a friendly animated airport story!

Leo is travelling to London for the first time, but he is not sure what to do at the airport. Emma helps him check in, get his boarding pass, find Gate 12, pass through security, and board his flight. Ollie joins the lesson with useful phrases and a quick quiz.

In this episode:
- Learn common airport vocabulary and expressions.
- Hear British English dialogue in context.
- Practise useful travel phrases.
- Test your understanding with a short quiz.

**Learning level:** Beginner (A1–A2)  
**Audio:** British English (en-GB)  
**Subtitles:** English and Afghan Dari  
**Format:** Animated story + listening practice + vocabulary review
