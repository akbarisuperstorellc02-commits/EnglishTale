# Character Bible

The following are original, fictional characters. These specifications are the canonical designs for every episode. Any generated image that conflicts with them is a draft only and must not be used in the final video.

## Leo — learner / main character
- Fictional young adult, approximately 19.
- Soft, slightly tousled brown hair; expressive brown eyes.
- **Blue hoodie**, dark blue jeans, white sneakers.
- Curious, kind, sometimes nervous in unfamiliar situations.
- Learns language by trying, making small mistakes, and improving.
- Default body language: open posture, expressive hands, readable facial expressions.

## Emma — supportive friend
- Fictional young adult, approximately 19.
- Shoulder-length brown hair; expressive brown eyes.
- **Pink sweater**, dark jeans, white sneakers.
- Calm, encouraging, practical, friendly.
- Helps Leo without sounding like a lecturer.

## Ollie — lesson guide
- **A small owl mascot, not a dog or other animal.**
- Warm brown feathers, cream-colored face and chest, large expressive eyes, round glasses, navy-blue scarf with a small golden accent.
- Cheerful, concise, encouraging.
- Appears mainly in lesson recap and quiz sections.
- Do not make Ollie resemble an existing branded character.

## Airport agent
- Fictional adult airport employee.
- Professional neutral uniform in navy and light blue, no real airline logos.
- Polite, calm, helpful.

## Continuity rules
1. These descriptions override any inconsistent concept artwork or generated draft.
2. Never change clothing, hair, eye color, age impression, species, accessories, or body proportions between shots in the same episode.
3. Leo must wear a blue hoodie; Emma must wear a pink sweater; Ollie must remain a small owl.
4. Do not add logos, brand marks, readable signage, or text unless manually added in post-production.
5. Use the same approved seed/reference image when available.
6. For two-character shots, provide both approved reference images if supported.
7. Inspect hands, faces, clothing, and background artifacts before accepting a generation.
8. Do not mark a character reference as approved until it matches this bible exactly.
