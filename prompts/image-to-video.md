# Episode 001 — Image-to-Video Motion Prompts

Use the approved still image as the input/reference. Generate short 4–6 second clips. Preserve character identity, outfit, body proportions, and scene layout. Avoid unnecessary dramatic movement.

## Airport establishing shot
“Slow cinematic camera push-in through the airport terminal. Distant travelers move naturally in the background, subtle airplane movement outside the windows, gentle ambient motion. Stable architecture, no warping, no new foreground characters.”

## Leo confused
“Leo looks left, then right, blinks naturally, and slightly adjusts his suitcase handle. His expression shows mild confusion, not fear. Slow gentle camera push-in. Preserve exact face, hair, hoodie, jeans, and shoes.”

## Emma reassures Leo
“Emma smiles warmly, gives a small reassuring nod, and gestures gently toward the check-in area. Leo looks at her and relaxes slightly. Natural subtle facial expressions and hand movement. Keep both character designs unchanged.”

## Check-in conversation
“Airport agent makes a small polite welcoming gesture. Leo nods and carefully hands over his passport. Emma waits beside him. Natural restrained movement, stable counter and background. Do not generate readable text.”

## Suitcase belt
“Suitcase moves slowly along the baggage belt. Airport agent makes a small pointing gesture. Leo watches attentively. Keep suitcase shape and character designs consistent.”

## Gate screen
“Leo and Emma look up at the information screen, then Emma points gently toward the left. Camera makes a subtle over-shoulder move. Keep screen text abstract and unreadable.”

## Walking
“Leo and Emma walk naturally side by side at a relaxed pace. Suitcase wheels roll smoothly. Camera tracks gently with them. No sudden turns, no outfit changes, no extra limbs.”

## Security
“Leo calmly places his bag into the tray and takes one small step forward. Security officer gestures toward the scanner. Keep the scene simple, safe, and non-dramatic.”

## Boarding goodbye
“Leo smiles and waves once. Emma smiles and gives a small wave back. Airplane remains visible through the windows. Gentle cinematic camera pull-back. Preserve exact character designs.”

## Ollie recap
“Ollie blinks, smiles, and makes a small welcoming wing gesture toward the empty board. Subtle body movement, steady camera. Keep glasses, scarf, feather colors, and proportions unchanged.”

## General negative constraints
No morphing, no face drift, no clothing changes, no extra fingers or limbs, no duplicated characters, no flickering, no text generation, no logos, no camera shake, no abrupt zooms.
