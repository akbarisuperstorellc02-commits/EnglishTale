## D. Audio
- [ ] Record/generate English dialogue separately by character

- [ ] Save clean WAV masters and compressed editing copies

## E. Edit and export
- [ ] Assemble the full episode in 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps
- [ ] Add English captions and synchronized Persian explanations/subtitles

- [ ] Mix dialogue clearly above music
- [ ] Watch the complete export on a phone with headphones and speaker
- [ ] Check subtitle timing, spelling, transitions, and audio clipping
- [ ] Export final MP4 and archive the project file

## F. Publish
- [ ] Final thumbnail reviewed at mobile size
- [ ] Title, description, tags, and playlist checked
- [ ] AI/synthetic-content disclosures reviewed against current YouTube rules
- [ ] Rights and license log complete
- [ ] Upload and verify playback, captions, and thumbnail
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 useful Shorts from the finished episode

## Release gate

Episode 001 is not ready to publish until sections B–F are complete and all accepted media assets have documented commercial-use permission. Repository documentation alone is not a finished video.
