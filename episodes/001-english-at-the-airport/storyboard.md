## Shot composition rules
- Keep important faces away from subtitle safe areas.
- Leave negative space for captions when generating images.
- Do not ask image models to render readable text; add all words in editing.
- Use a mixture of wide, medium, and close shots.
- Avoid rapid scene changes during beginner listening lines.
