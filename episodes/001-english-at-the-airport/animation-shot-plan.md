## Character asset assignment

## Edit and sound rules
1. English dialogue is heard first; Persian explanation follows without overlapping the English line.
2. Keep English captions visible during English speech. Use a separate Persian caption track.
3. Do not place subtitles over faces or important hand actions.
4. Use short, gentle transitions; avoid fast cuts during listening practice.
5. Add airport ambience quietly under dialogue; duck it during speech.
6. The supplied SVG rigs use procedural placeholder mouth movement. Final mouth timing must be aligned to recorded dialogue before export.

## Quality gate
- Check the Gate 12 wording against the script in every shot.
- Confirm Leo's blue hoodie, Emma's pink sweater, and Ollie's owl design remain consistent.
- Check all English spelling and Dari translation before rendering.
- Watch the complete export once with sound and once muted to catch visual timing issues.
