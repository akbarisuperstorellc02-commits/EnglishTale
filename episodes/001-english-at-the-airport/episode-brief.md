# Episode 001 — English at the Airport

## Core idea
Leo is taking his first trip to London. At the airport he feels unsure about check-in, security, and finding his gate. Emma helps him, and he successfully boards his flight.

## Learning objectives
By the end, learners should be able to understand and use:
- Where is the check-in counter?
- I'm flying to London.
- May I see your passport, please?
- Here you are.
- I have one suitcase.
- Where is Gate 12?
- Please put your bag on the tray.
- Please walk through the scanner.
- Boarding is now open.
- Have a wonderful trip!

## Language level
A1 with a few A2 expressions. Explain unfamiliar vocabulary briefly.

## Runtime plan
Target 7:00. The script should be paced naturally, not artificially stretched:
- 00:00–00:20 hook
- 00:20–01:00 arrival and setup
- 01:00–02:20 check-in
- 02:20–03:15 finding the gate
- 03:15–04:15 security
- 04:15–05:15 boarding
- 05:15–06:15 phrase recap and listen-repeat
- 06:15–07:00 quiz and close

The exact final runtime depends on voice performance, pauses, and animation timing. Adjust the edit to land between 6 and 8 minutes.

## Accuracy notes
- A boarding pass is not simply the same thing as a ticket; teach it as «کارت پرواز».
- Airport procedures vary by country and airline. This is a simplified learning story, not travel or security advice.
- Keep airport staff polite and the scene family-friendly.
