## Short 01 — Check in at the airport

- Hook text: "Your first airport check-in? Say this!"
- Learning goal: answer where you are flying, show your passport, and talk about checked baggage.
- Key English lines:
  - "Where are you flying today?"
  - "I'm flying to London."
  - "May I see your passport, please?"
  - "Do you have any bags to check in?"
- Afghan Dari support: show concise translations beneath each English line.
- Visual focus: airport agent, Leo, passport, and suitcase.
- End CTA: "Save this for your next trip!"
