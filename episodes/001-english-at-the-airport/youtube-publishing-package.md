## Description

Learn useful British English for travelling through a simple airport story!

Leo is travelling to London for the first time. Emma helps him check in, show his passport, get a boarding pass, find Gate 12, pass through security, and board his flight. Join Ollie for a quick phrase review, listen-and-repeat practice, and a short quiz.

**In this A1 beginner English lesson, you will practise:**
- Where is the check-in counter?
- May I see your passport, please?
- Here you are.
- Where is Gate 12?
- Have a wonderful trip!

🎧 Listen to clear British English dialogue.
📖 Afghan Dari subtitles are available as an optional subtitle track.
🗣️ Repeat the useful phrases and test what you learned.
